AM I A POET?

CURRENTLY IN THE ATTEMPT.

Poets are often romanticised by society because they are assumed to have an inherent capability that is unique to them. Some name it creativity, some intelligence, and since we could not come to a conclusion, we named it being 'poetic'. Hence, most people, atleast once in their lives try their luck at writing, and so did I as a child to perhaps accidentally discover myself as one of the special few capable. Unfortunately nothing about my piece of writing was anywhere near poetic.

As I grew up, experiencing life head on and observing the lives of the people around me as well, I began growing perspectives. In my journey as a writer, these perspectives led me to poetry. Perhaps the tragedy that my neighbour endured, or a celebration at a friend's house, or even my seniors' adventurous romantic tales on bus rides to school, ended up on my notepad.

Sometimes with rhymes, mostly without, such experiences through empathetic perspectives led to narrations that were somehow 'poetic'. Though no one really knows how to exactly define whats poetry and judge whats not, for now let it be the subject of society's belief regarding inherent intellectuals.


WHAT LANGUAGES

DO I WRITE IN?

ENGLISH

HINDI

URDU


Though I am a Bengali, by birth, upbringing and geography, I write in only two languages: English & Hindi/Urdu. I know Hindi and Urdu are different languages but as Javed Akhtar once said that the difference between the two for the common man is that the portion he/she understands is Hindi and the rest that sounds familiar- Urdu.

I have never been exposed to Urdu as a language but have loved and been proficient in Hindi for years because of a Hindi-speaking friend group. Hence I take fancy Urdu words which I understand because of Bollywood and Shayars and fill the grammatical gaps with Hindi.
