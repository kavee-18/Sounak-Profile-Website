CINEMA,

WHERE DO WE EVEN BEGIN?

Cinema was my first love. I remember every movie I have ever seen, every song from it, every dialogue that matters. When we're asked to introduce ourselves as kids, the line describing our hobbies is informally almost mandatory, and every kid who is not old enough to logically have hobbies, would mention "reading books" and "watching movies". That was never the case for me because I hated reading books and 'watching movies' could never be a proxy answer to me.

The first memory I have of cinema is going a small single screen in my town for a regional Bengali movie. I remember the tiger print makeup on the actors in it, and the taste of the 5 rupee cake my father boought us from the stall at the interval. Eventually I progressed to the only multiplex in my town, with my first film being 'Happy New Year'. I remember seeing all the posters on the corridors and wondering whether one ticket lets us watch all of them one after the other, not because I didn't enjoy the film I was currently watching, but because I didn't want the experience to end.

I don't remember skipping a single movie on cable broadcast. Considering we are in the age of OTTs now, you would imagine great inconvenience at not being able to play/ pause at your will, not being able to rewind, watching half the broadcast and connecting the plot when you watch the other half a week later when it comes on again, but no, none of that mattered to the kid looking at the screen with wonder, imagining himself in those worlds when the screen goes off.
