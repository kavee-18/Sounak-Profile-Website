HOW DID THIS GUY

END UP AT

IIM BANGALORE?

I have always been fascinated by entrepreneurship and the world around it. Entrepreneurship seemed to be a niche subject in terms of its availability in educational institutions in India, and hence I was presented with the struggle of finding an appropriate institute that provided this exact niche specialization that I was so fascinated with. This is when IIM Bangalore came to the rescue.


IIM Bangalore has been a landmark institute globally in terms of its impact in the field of management studies. It recognised the need for widespread education in entrepreneurship and introduced an online asynchronous degree-BBA DBE(Digital Business and Entrepreneurship) in 2024. When I came across this opportunity, I did not need to think twice. I was so firm in my ambition to pursue this degree, I did not register for a single generalized national entrance test like CUET or IPMAT. I was firm that I would only attend college if it benefitted my aspirations, and hence risked an entire academic year for the one that did.


Fortunately, I scored 49/60 in the BBA DBE Entrance Test and was one of the highest scorers this year according to sample data available online. The batch officially started on 8th September, 2026, following the orientations and faculty addresses with a batch size of 1400+ students.
